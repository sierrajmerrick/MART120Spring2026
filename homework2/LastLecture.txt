Watching “The Last Lecture” speech by Randy Pausch was very inspirational. He seemed to have an incredible outlook on life, and it was very admirable to listen to him talk about various aspects of his life with such a positive perspective. I think this video was the best possible example of someone with a growth oriented mindset. His perspective on dreams and what it really means to achieve them is both clever and inspiring. I think the quote “experience is what you get when you didn’t get what you wanted” stood out to me and affected me the most. It is very admirable to hear someone say such an influential statement in such a simple way. I think it is really important to be grateful for the process of trial and error, and to find appreciation in the opportunity for a learning experience that comes from failure. I really identified with his message about “brick walls are there to show us how badly we want something”. To me, my biggest accomplishments in life are not anything extraordinary, but things I have accomplished against the odds. This kind of mindset has pushed me to take risks and work hard to get the things I want, even when it feels very difficult and impractical. Over time, these experiences build on each other and it seems easier and easier to convince myself I am capable of doing the work to get something I want.
